Accepted

Pager Bot posts alerts to Slack and lets responders acknowledge from chat.

## Context

## Decision Drivers

- Visibility
- Low friction
- Acknowledge in chat

## Considered Options

Missed.

### Option B: Slack bot (chosen)
Visible.

## Decision Rationale

Chat is where responders live.

## Consequences
